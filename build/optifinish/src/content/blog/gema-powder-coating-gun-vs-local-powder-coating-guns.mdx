## Technical Advantages That Drive the Difference

### PowerBoost Technology (Up to 110 kV)
GEMA's PowerBoost® technology in the OptiFlex Pro series delivers industry-leading electrostatic voltage. Higher voltage means better powder wrap-around on complex parts — more surface covered per pass, less manual touch-up required.

### Precision Current and Voltage Control
GEMA guns allow independent adjustment of voltage and current. This is critical when coating different powder chemistries — standard polyester, epoxy-polyester, or specialty powders — without changing guns or damaging the equipment.

### Automated Cleaning on Color Change
With GEMA guns, automated cleaning of powder-contact components during color change reduces downtime and contamination risk. Local guns require fully manual cleaning, which takes longer and depends heavily on operator discipline.

## When a Local Gun Might Be Acceptable

We recommend local guns only in specific, limited situations:

- As backup guns for emergency situations when a primary GEMA gun is in for service
- For very low-volume, non-critical coating tasks where finish quality standards are minimal
- For plants in early stages that intend to upgrade once production volume justifies it

For any plant where coating quality matters — which is most of our customers — the GEMA gun is the right investment.
