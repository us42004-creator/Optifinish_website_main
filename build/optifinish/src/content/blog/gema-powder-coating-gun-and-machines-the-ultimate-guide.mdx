## How GEMA Powder Coating Guns Work

GEMA powder coating guns use electrostatic technology to charge powder particles and attract them to a grounded metal workpiece. The process:

1. **Powder is fed** from a hopper or OptiCenter through a hose to the gun
2. **Inside the gun**, the powder passes through an electrostatic charging zone where high-voltage corona or triboelectric charging imparts an electrostatic charge
3. **Charged particles** are propelled by compressed air toward the grounded workpiece
4. **Electrostatic attraction** causes particles to deposit on the metal surface, including recessed areas (Faraday cage penetration)
5. The powder-coated part is then **cured in an oven** to form the final film

This mechanism is fundamental to powder coating's high transfer efficiency and uniform coverage — advantages that liquid paint cannot match.

## GEMA Powder Coating Machines (Control Units)

The control unit (sometimes called the powder coating machine or generator) is the power supply and control center for the gun system. GEMA control units:

- Regulate high-voltage output to the gun
- Control powder feed rate and air settings
- Enable recipe programming for different parts and powder types
- Provide fault detection and diagnostics
- Connect to centralised plant control systems (for automatic lines)

Selecting the right control unit is as important as selecting the gun — the combination must be matched to your application requirements.
