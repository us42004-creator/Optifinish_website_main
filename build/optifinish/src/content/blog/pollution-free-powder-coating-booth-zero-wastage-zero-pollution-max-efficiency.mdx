Powder coating is already one of the most environmentally responsible industrial finishing processes available — but only when the booth system is properly designed. A poorly engineered booth can allow powder to escape into the workshop, waste expensive material, and create compliance problems for Indian manufacturers subject to environmental regulations.

GEMA powder coating booths, supplied and installed by OptiFinish (Value Added Coating Solutions Pvt. Ltd.) across India, are designed from the ground up to prevent powder escape, minimise wastage, and maintain a clean, compliant operating environment.

## No Powder Escaping the Booth

The defining characteristic of a GEMA powder coating booth is its closed-system design. Controlled airflow, combined with precision-engineered booth geometry, ensures that powder particles remain inside the booth during the coating process. This is achieved through:

- **Calibrated negative pressure** — air is drawn inward, preventing any powder from migrating into the surrounding workshop
- **Filter systems** — multi-stage filtration captures all overspray before air is discharged
- **Sealed construction** — no gaps or leakage points that would allow powder escape

For Indian manufacturing facilities where workshop air quality affects both worker health and product quality on adjacent lines, this containment is essential.

## Zero Wastage Through Closed-Loop Recovery

In conventional powder coating setups, overspray that misses the part is lost. In a GEMA booth, that overspray is captured, filtered, and returned to the powder supply. This closed-loop recovery system means:

- **Reclaimed powder is reused** — significantly reducing powder consumption per part
- **No expensive powder dumped** after color changes (with proper clean-out procedures)
- **Lower material cost per square metre** of coating applied

For Indian plant operators where powder cost represents 30–50% of total coating cost, this recovery efficiency directly improves margins.
