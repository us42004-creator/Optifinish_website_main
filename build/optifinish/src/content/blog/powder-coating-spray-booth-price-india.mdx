## What Does a Powder Coating Spray Booth Do?

A spray booth serves several functions simultaneously:

- **Contains overspray** — prevents powder from migrating into the surrounding workshop environment
- **Recovers unused powder** — captures overspray and returns it to the supply system, reducing material waste
- **Controls airflow** — maintains the correct air velocity and direction for efficient powder application and operator safety
- **Provides the coating environment** — correct grounding, lighting, and access for gun operation

A poorly designed or undersized booth creates problems across all of these functions, leading to higher powder waste, worse finish quality, and non-compliant operating conditions.

## Parts of a Powder Coating Booth

Every spray booth — whether manual or automatic — has two functional zones:

### Powder Spraying Area
The main chamber where parts are coated. The part hangs on a jig connected to a grounded conveyor or stand. The operator or automatic gun applies the charged powder. Airflow is directed to carry overspray toward the recovery system.

### Powder Recovery Area
The section where overspray powder is captured by a filter or cyclone system, sieved to remove agglomerates, and returned to the powder supply hopper. Effective recovery reduces powder consumption significantly — in a well-designed system, recovery efficiency exceeds 95%.

## How the Booth Manages Airflow

Proper airflow balance is critical to booth performance:

- **Exhaust fan** draws air (and suspended powder) out of the spraying zone toward the filters
- **Supply air or compensating fan** provides fresh replacement air at the booth opening, maintaining slight negative pressure inside the booth
- Running both fans in balance keeps powder inside the booth, prevents contamination of the workshop, and creates safe working conditions for operators

In Indian manufacturing environments where dust and humidity can be significant, booth airflow management also protects the powder from contamination.

## Powder Coating Booth Types Available in India

### Manual Open-Face Booth
A simple, open-face enclosure with manual gun access. Suitable for small batch jobs, job shops, and operations with diverse part types.
- Lowest capital cost
- Most flexible for different part sizes
- Requires good operator discipline for consistent results

### Enclosed Manual Booth with Recovery
A fully enclosed booth with integrated powder recovery (cyclone or filter system). Suitable for medium-volume manual operations where material cost justifies recovery investment.

### GEMA Magic System Automatic Booth
The GEMA Magic System is a fully automatic booth system designed for high-volume conveyorised lines with quick colour change requirements. ## Powder Coating Booth Price in India

Booth pricing in India depends on several variables:

- **Booth dimensions** — determined by part size (height, width, depth)
- **Construction material** — mild steel with paint, stainless steel, or special coatings
- **Recovery system type** — manual filter, cartridge filter, or cyclone with after-filter
- **Automation level** — manual, semi-automatic with reciprocator, or fully automatic GEMA system
- **Gun count** — number of gun positions in the booth

As a rough guide for Indian buyers in 2025:
- Basic manual booth with filter recovery: from ₹2–5 lakhs
- Enclosed manual booth with cartridge filter: ₹5–12 lakhs
- GEMA automatic booth (Magic System) with full integration: ₹25 lakhs and above, depending on configuration

Note that booth pricing is always part of a complete plant cost — it cannot be evaluated in isolation from the conveyor, oven, and gun systems it integrates with.
