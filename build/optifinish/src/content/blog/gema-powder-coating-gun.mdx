## How a Powder Coating Gun Works

The electrostatic gun charges powder particles as they pass through the gun barrel. In a corona-discharge gun — the most common type — a high-voltage electrode at the gun tip creates an electrostatic field. Powder particles moving through this field acquire a charge and are propelled by compressed air toward the grounded metal workpiece.

The electrostatic charge causes particles to be attracted to the metal surface, including recessed areas and undercuts. This "wrap-around" effect is one of the key advantages of electrostatic powder coating over liquid paint — a single pass can achieve significant coverage on complex geometries.

## Types of GEMA Powder Coating Guns

### Manual Powder Coating Guns

Manual guns are held and operated by an operator, making them ideal for:
- Smaller production batches
- Complex or irregular part geometries that require operator judgment
- Touch-up work after automatic coating
- Job shops handling diverse part types and sizes

GEMA manual guns — including the OptiFlex Pro and OptiSelect series — are designed for ergonomic all-day use, with balanced weight and intuitive controls. The powder output control is located on the gun handle, allowing the operator to adjust settings without returning to the control unit — a time-saving feature that local gun alternatives do not typically offer.

### Automatic Powder Coating Guns

Automatic guns are fixed to reciprocators or robotic arms and operate without direct operator involvement. They are engineered for continuous high-volume production:
- Consistent spray pattern across thousands of parts per shift
- High-cycle durability without performance degradation
- Multiple nozzle options for different part profiles
- Integration with GEMA MagicControl systems for automatic speed synchronisation with the conveyor

Automatic guns are the backbone of any conveyorised powder coating line, and the GEMA OptiGun range is the standard choice for such applications in India.

## Key Technologies in GEMA Guns

### Corona Charging
The standard charging method in GEMA guns. A high-voltage electrode creates an ionised field at the gun tip, charging powder particles as they pass through. GEMA corona guns achieve up to 110 kV with PowerBoost technology in the OptiFlex Pro series.

### Tribo Charging
In a tribo gun, powder acquires a charge through friction contact with a specially designed gun barrel interior, eliminating the high-voltage electrode. Tribo charging is used for certain specialty applications where back-ionisation or Faraday cage effects are a significant problem.

### Digital Valve Control (DVC)
GEMA's patented DVC technology uses motor-driven valves to regulate compressed air to the injector, providing precise and reproducible powder output control. This results in consistent film thickness — critical for quality-conscious Indian manufacturers.

### PCC Mode (Precise Charge Control)
Available in advanced GEMA gun models, PCC mode regulates spray current with high accuracy in the very low ampere range (0–10 µA). This prevents overcharging of difficult powders — metallics, textures, and thick-build systems — ensuring a clean finish without orange peel or back-ionisation artefacts.

The right gun configuration depends on your application:

- **Entry-level fabricator:** A basic manual GEMA gun with standard hopper — reliable quality at a manageable investment
