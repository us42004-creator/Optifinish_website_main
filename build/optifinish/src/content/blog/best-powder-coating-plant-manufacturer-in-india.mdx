## What Is Powder Coating?

Powder coating is an industrial finishing process in which a dry powder — a fine blend of resin and pigment — is electrostatically applied to a prepared metal surface and then cured in an oven. The result is a hard, continuous, solvent-free coating that outperforms liquid paint on durability, environmental compliance, and operational efficiency.

The process takes place in a controlled environment, usually involving three main steps: surface pre-treatment (cleaning), powder application (spray booth), and thermal curing (oven).

## Components of a Complete Powder Coating Plant

A powder coating plant manufactured by OptiFinish typically comprises the following main sections:

### 1. Pre-Treatment (PT) Line

The PT line is the chemical cleaning and surface preparation section. It removes oils, rust, and mill scale from metal substrates and applies a phosphate conversion coating that improves adhesion and corrosion resistance.

Our PT lines are custom-designed based on:
- The metal being coated (mild steel, galvanised steel, aluminium)
- Required corrosion performance (indoor vs. outdoor applications)
- Production volume and part geometry

Standard stages include degreasing, water rinse, iron or zinc phosphating, and passivation.

### 2. Powder Coating Booth

The spray booth is the enclosure where powder is applied to the grounded parts. OptiFinish manufactures custom steel booths and also supplies GEMA automatic booths with integrated powder recovery.

A well-designed booth ensures:
- Controlled airflow that contains overspray inside the enclosure
- Efficient powder recovery and return to the supply circuit
- Easy access for cleaning and colour changes
- Compliance with Indian environmental norms for finishing operations

### 3. Curing Oven

After powder application, parts enter the curing oven where the powder melts, flows, and chemically crosslinks at temperatures of 160–200°C. OptiFinish designs and fabricates custom curing ovens for Indian clients — in batch configurations for small plants and conveyorised tunnel configurations for continuous lines.

Key design parameters: part dimensions, required dwell time at temperature, production throughput, and energy efficiency.

### 4. Conveyor System

For semi-automatic and fully automatic plants, an overhead conveyor transports parts continuously through the PT line, dry-off oven, spray booth, and curing oven at a controlled line speed. Conveyor design is determined by part weight, hanging jig geometry, and required throughput.

### 5. Powder Coating Guns and Powder Management

OptiFinish supplies GEMA powder coating guns — both manual and automatic — as part of complete plant installations. For automatic lines, GEMA's OptiCenter powder management system handles powder feeding, recovery, and sieving in an integrated unit.
