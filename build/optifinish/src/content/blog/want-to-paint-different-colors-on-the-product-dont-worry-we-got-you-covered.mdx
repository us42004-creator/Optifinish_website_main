Slow color changes mean lost production time. Poor cleaning means costly cross-contamination rejects. GEMA's Quick Color Change booth systems solve both problems — a major reason why Indian powder coating plant operators choose GEMA over conventional booth designs.

## The Challenge of Multi-Color Coating

In a typical Indian powder coating job shop, customer orders arrive in mixed colors — RAL 9003 white for one batch, RAL 6005 green for agricultural equipment, RAL 1023 yellow for safety products, and so on. Every color change is a potential source of:

- **Downtime** — cleaning the booth and guns between batches
- **Contamination** — residual powder from the previous color contaminating the next batch
- **Powder waste** — recovered mixed powder that cannot be reused

GEMA's approach addresses all three.

## How GEMA Magic System Enables Quick Color Change

### Fully Customisable Booth Layout
Magic Systems can be configured with different booth dimensions, air volume settings, number of guns, and touch-up station positions. This means every installation is optimised for the specific parts and production mix of that plant — not a generic configuration.

### Efficient Powder Application
The booth's airflow and gun positioning are engineered for maximum first-pass transfer efficiency. Less powder misses the part, which means:

- Less powder to clean from the booth between colors
- Lower powder consumption per part
- Reduced downtime during color changes

### Sieving and Fresh Powder Addition in One Step
Recovered overspray is sieved and blended with fresh powder in a single automated step. This maintains consistent powder properties and color fidelity across a full production run.

### Non-Conductive Walls Stay Clean
Magic System walls do not accumulate powder during operation. When a color change is needed, the booth wipes clean in minutes — not hours. This is the single biggest factor in reducing color change downtime in Indian powder coating plants.

## Practical Color Change Time

With a properly configured GEMA Magic System, color change time in Indian powder coating operations typically runs:

- **5–15 minutes** for a basic color change on a single-gun manual setup
- **15–30 minutes** for a full automatic booth cleaning and changeover

Compare this to 1–2 hours with conventional booth designs, and the productivity gain over a multi-shift operation is substantial.
