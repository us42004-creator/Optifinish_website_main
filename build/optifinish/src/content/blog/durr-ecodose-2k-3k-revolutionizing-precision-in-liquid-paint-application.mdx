Even small deviations in the mixing ratio between resin and hardener — or between base coat, catalyst, and effect coat — result in adhesion failures, incorrect gloss, or premature coating breakdown. The Dürr EcoDose 2K and 3K dosing systems solve this problem with engineered precision at industrial throughput rates.

## What Is the Dürr EcoDose System?

The EcoDose is a gear pump-based volumetric dosing system for multi-component liquid paint systems. It measures and delivers each paint component — base, hardener, effect additives — in precisely controlled ratios, regardless of viscosity variations or supply pressure fluctuations.

### EcoDose 2K — Two-Component Dosing

The EcoDose 2K is designed for standard two-component paint systems:
- **Typical application:** 2K polyurethane, 2K epoxy, 2K acrylic topcoats
- **Ratio accuracy:** ±1% deviation from set mixing ratio
- **Flow range:** Customised to line requirements
- **Material compatibility:** Suitable for solvent-borne, water-borne, and high-solids paint formulations

### EcoDose 3K — Three-Component Dosing

The EcoDose 3K adds a third component channel for applications requiring a separate effect component, accelerator, or specialty additive:
- **Typical application:** Effect coats (metallic, pearl), tri-component industrial topcoats
- **Same precision standard** as the 2K but with the added complexity of a three-way ratio

## Why Precision Dosing Matters in Indian Manufacturing

Indian manufacturers supplying to automotive OEMs, export markets, or premium consumer segments face increasing quality requirements for liquid coating systems. The consequences of poor dosing include:

- **Adhesion failure** — incorrect hardener ratio prevents full crosslinking
- **Gloss deviation** — off-ratio material cures to different surface energy
- **Rework and scrap** — costly in high-value components
- **Customer complaints and returns** — particularly severe for export-facing manufacturers

The EcoDose eliminates dosing error as a variable, placing the quality control responsibility squarely on paint formulation and application technique — both of which are much easier to manage.
