## Why Manual Guns Still Matter

Even in highly automated powder coating plants, manual guns serve critical roles:

- **Touch-up and rework** on parts that missed full coverage in the automatic zone
- **Small batch and custom work** where setting up automatic reciprocators is not economical
- **Complex profiles and recesses** where automatic guns cannot reach consistently
- **R&D and sampling** before committing to full production runs

For Indian job shops and fabricators handling diverse part types, a high-quality manual gun is a daily necessity.

## Automated Color Change — Minimising Downtime

GEMA manual gun systems are designed for fast, clean color changes. All components in direct contact with powder — nozzle, electrode, and powder tube — are cleaned quickly and automatically while the operator replaces the powder box. This means:

- Color change time measured in minutes, not hours
- No cross-contamination between batches
- Consistent results from the first shot after each changeover

In Indian contract coating operations where multiple color batches run in a single shift, this automated cleaning capability is a direct productivity multiplier.

## Maximum Coating Power

GEMA manual guns are rated for maximum coating power designed to handle the full range of powder types used in Indian industry, including:

- Standard thermosetting polyester powders
- Epoxy-polyester hybrid powders
- Pure epoxy powders for internal protective coatings
- Textured and metallic finishes popular in architectural aluminium and furniture sectors

The consistent electrostatic charge generated by GEMA guns ensures excellent powder adhesion and wrap-around effect — critical for complex parts like racking uprights, automotive brackets, and HVAC components.

## Precise Control for Challenging Applications

GEMA manual guns provide operators with fine control over:

- **Voltage and current settings** — adjustable independently for different powder chemistries
- **Powder flow rate** — controlling film thickness on the fly
- **Air volume** — tuning spray pattern for recessed vs. flat surfaces

This level of control is what separates professional-grade GEMA equipment from generic powder coating guns available in the Indian market. Operators trained on GEMA guns consistently achieve better first-pass transfer efficiency and fewer rejects.
