## Atomisation Quality

The defining performance parameter for any spray gun is atomisation quality — how finely and consistently the paint is broken into droplets before it reaches the substrate. Poor atomisation leads to rough or orange-peel surface texture, uneven film thickness, and higher paint consumption per unit area. Solvent entrapment from large droplets can also cause adhesion problems as the film cures.

The EcoGun 2100 AirCombi's precision air cap geometry ensures fine, consistent atomisation across a range of paint viscosities and application speeds. This is critical for Indian manufacturers working with diverse paint formulations and substrates — particularly those supplying to customers who specify finish quality standards in their supplier agreements.
