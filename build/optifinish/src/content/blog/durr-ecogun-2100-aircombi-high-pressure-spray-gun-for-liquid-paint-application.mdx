## Atomisation Quality

The defining performance parameter for any spray gun is atomisation quality — how finely and consistently the paint is broken into droplets. Poor atomisation leads to:

- Rough or "orange peel" surface texture
- Uneven film thickness
- Solvent entrapment causing adhesion problems
- Higher paint consumption per unit area

The EcoGun 2100 AirCombi's precision air cap geometry ensures fine, consistent atomisation across a range of paint viscosities and application speeds — critical for Indian manufacturers working with diverse paint formulations and substrates.
