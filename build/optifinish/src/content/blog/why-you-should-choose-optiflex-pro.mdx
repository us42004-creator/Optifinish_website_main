## What Makes the OptiFlex Pro Different?

### PowerBoost Technology — 110 kV Performance

GEMA's proprietary **PowerBoost® technology** allows the OptiFlex Pro to deliver up to 110 kV electrostatic output — the highest available in a manual powder coating gun. Higher voltage means stronger electrostatic attraction, which translates directly to better wrap-around effect and higher first-pass transfer efficiency. For Indian plant operators, this reduces powder waste per part and lowers the cost of each square metre coated. It also means better penetration into recessed areas — a practical advantage when coating complex profiles like racking systems, automotive brackets, and architectural aluminium sections where uniform coverage on every face and edge is non-negotiable.

### MagicControl 4.0 Digital Control Unit

The OptiFlex Pro is operated with GEMA's **MagicControl 4.0** unit, which stores coating parameters digitally for instant recall at the start of each production run. Voltage, current, powder flow, and air volume can all be set once for a given part type, saved to the unit's memory, and retrieved without any re-entry by the operator. This eliminates the variation that typically occurs when experienced operators rotate shifts or when new operators are trained, and it makes quality audits significantly simpler because every parameter is logged and traceable.

### Automated Colour Change

All components that contact powder — nozzle, electrode, and powder tube — can be cleaned quickly and automatically when switching between powder types or colours. This dramatically reduces downtime during colour changes compared to fully manual cleaning, which is the only option on local guns. Changeover that once took an hour can be reduced to a matter of minutes with the OptiFlex Pro setup. For Indian plants that run multiple colours within a single shift, this automated cleaning capability is a direct productivity multiplier with measurable impact on daily throughput.

### Independent Voltage and Current Control

Voltage and current settings are adjustable independently on the MagicControl 4.0, giving operators precise control over the electrostatic charge for different powder chemistries and part geometries. This is particularly important when moving between standard polyester powders and specialty finishes such as metallics or textures within the same production day. Incorrect current settings on metallics cause back-ionisation and surface defects; the OptiFlex Pro's precise charge control prevents these problems at the source.
