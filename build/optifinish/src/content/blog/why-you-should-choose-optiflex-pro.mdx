## What Makes the OptiFlex Pro Different?

### Closed-Hopper Powder Protection

The OptiFlex Pro features a closed hopper design that shields powder from ambient humidity and airborne contaminants. This gentle fluidisation mechanism ensures ideal powder delivery consistency — critical in the variable climate conditions common across Indian manufacturing hubs like Greater Noida, Pune, and Chennai.

### PowerBoost Technology — 110 kV Performance

GEMA's proprietary PowerBoost® technology allows the OptiFlex Pro to deliver up to **110 kV**, enabling superior wrap-around effect and first-pass transfer efficiency. For Indian powder coating plant operators, this directly translates to less powder waste and lower per-part coating costs.

Higher voltage also means better penetration into recessed areas — a key advantage when coating complex profiles like racking systems, automotive brackets, and architectural sections.

### Automated Color Change

All components that come into contact with powder can be cleaned quickly and automatically when switching powder boxes. This dramatically reduces downtime during color changes — a significant productivity advantage in Indian plants that run multiple colors per shift.
