## Why Powder Coating Is the Standard Finish for Almirahs in India

Powder coating has become the default finishing process for steel almirahs, wardrobes, and storage cabinets in India for several reasons:

- **Durability** — powder-coated steel resists scratches, impacts, and humidity far better than liquid paint or stove enamel
- **Wide colour range** — thousands of RAL and custom colours available in powder form
- **Consistent finish** — smooth, uniform film across all almirah panels
- **Environmental compliance** — no solvent emissions, important for manufacturers in industrial estates with pollution control requirements
- **Cost efficiency at scale** — lower per-part cost at medium-to-high volumes compared to liquid painting

## Types of Almirah Powder Coating Plants

### Manual Almirah Powder Coating Plant

A manual setup is the entry point for small-to-medium almirah manufacturers:

- **Components:** Spray booth, curing oven, manual GEMA powder coating gun, basic hopper, jigs for hanging almirah panels
- **Throughput:** 50–200 almirah sets per shift depending on panel sizes and operator efficiency
- **Investment (2025):** Approximately ₹8–20 lakhs depending on booth size and oven specification
- **Best for:** New entrants, small manufacturers, or plants with diverse part types needing manual flexibility

### Semi-Automatic Almirah Powder Coating Line

A semi-automatic setup adds a basic conveyor and may include a vertical reciprocator for the spray zone:

- **Components:** Pre-treatment spray system, conveyor, automatic spray booth with reciprocator, curing oven
- **Throughput:** 200–500 almirah sets per shift
- **Investment (2025):** ₹20–50 lakhs
- **Best for:** Growing manufacturers scaling from manual to automatic operation

### Fully Automatic Conveyorised Almirah Line

The fully automatic setup is standard for large almirah manufacturers in India producing high volumes of standardised panel sizes:

- **Components:** Fully automatic PT spray system, continuous overhead conveyor, automatic powder coating booth with GEMA OptiGun guns and reciprocators, large curing oven
- **Throughput:** 500–2000+ almirah sets per shift
- **Investment (2025):** ₹50 lakhs to ₹1.5 crore+
- **Best for:** Established manufacturers with high and consistent volume

## The Almirah Powder Coating Process

A properly configured almirah powder coating line processes panels through the following stages:

### Stage 1: Pre-Treatment
Almirah steel panels are cleaned of mill scale, rust prevention oil, and welding residues. A phosphating conversion coating is applied for corrosion protection and powder adhesion.

### Stage 2: Drying
After pre-treatment, panels are dried in a dry-off oven before powder application. Residual moisture causes powder adhesion problems.

### Stage 3: Powder Application
Panels hang on jigs and pass through or are placed in the spray booth. GEMA powder coating guns — manual or automatic — apply the charged powder to all panel surfaces.

### Stage 4: Curing
Powder-coated panels cure in the oven at 180–200°C. Typical cure time for standard polyester powder on almirah panels: 15 minutes at temperature.

### Stage 5: Cooling and Packing
Cured panels cool before inspection, assembly, and packing.
