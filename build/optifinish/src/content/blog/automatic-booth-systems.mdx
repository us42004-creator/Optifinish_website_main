## Single-Color and Quick Color Change Configurations

GEMA offers booth systems for two primary production models:

### Single-Color High-Volume Lines

For plants dedicated to one color — common in automotive, electrical enclosure, and agricultural equipment manufacturing — GEMA's single-channel booth design maximises throughput with minimal complexity. The compact footprint makes it suitable for Indian factories where floor space is at a premium.

### Quick Color Change (Magic System)

The GEMA Magic System is engineered for operations that need to switch colors rapidly and cleanly. Key features include:

- **Pre/post touch-up zones** integrated with the automatic application area
- **Easy customisation** of booth dimensions, air volume, gun count, and touch-up station positions
- **Efficient powder recovery** with sieving and fresh powder addition in a single step
- **Fast cleaning cycles** that minimise production interruption between batches

## Non-Conductive Walls for Cleaner Operation

One of GEMA Magic System's most operationally important features is the non-conductive wall construction. These walls:

- Stay clean during coating operation, without powder buildup
- Reduce cleaning time between color changes to minutes rather than hours
- Ensure no cross-contamination between color batches — critical for premium industrial finishes

In Indian plants where color discipline is essential (for example, in aluminium extrusion coating or contract coating services), this is a significant quality assurance advantage.

## Compact and Flexible Design

The GEMA automatic booth is designed with a versatile concept combining:

- A pre/post touch-up zone for operator-assisted finish correction
- A large automatic application area using reciprocators and automatic guns
- A compact overall layout suited to existing factory buildings

This means Indian manufacturers can integrate a high-performance automatic booth into facilities that were originally set up for manual coating, without major civil works.

## Powder Recovery and Efficiency

All GEMA automatic booth systems incorporate closed-loop powder recovery. Overspray is captured, sieved, and returned to the hopper automatically. For Indian powder coating plants processing large volumes, this directly reduces consumable costs and keeps the coating environment clean and compliant.
