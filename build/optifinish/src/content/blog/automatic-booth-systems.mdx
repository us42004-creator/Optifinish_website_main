GEMA automatic booth systems remove that constraint — delivering consistent coating quality, rapid colour changes, and substantially reduced powder waste whether you coat a single colour or a full spectrum of RAL shades through the working day.

## Single-Colour and Quick Colour Change Configurations

GEMA offers booth systems designed around two distinct production models, and the right choice depends on how your plant actually runs. Both configurations are built on the same principle: [GEMA OptiGun automatic guns](/products/gema/automatic-gun) mounted on [reciprocators](/products/gema/reciprocators) move in programmed vertical strokes as parts pass through on a conveyor, delivering consistent application without operator involvement in the spray zone.

### Single-Colour High-Volume Lines

For plants dedicated to a single colour — common in automotive component manufacturing, electrical enclosure coating, and agricultural equipment finishing — GEMA's single-channel booth design maximises throughput with minimal operational complexity. The compact footprint makes this configuration suitable for Indian factories where floor space is a genuine constraint. Simplified controls and a reduced component count also lower the maintenance demands on the plant team, which matters considerably in operations where dedicated maintenance staff are limited.

### Quick Colour Change — The Magic System

The GEMA Magic System is engineered for operations that need to switch colours rapidly and cleanly without extended production interruptions. It combines pre- and post-application touch-up zones with a large automatic application area, allowing operator-assisted finish correction around the automatic spray zone. Booth dimensions, air volume, gun count, and touch-up station positions can all be configured to suit the specific plant layout. Fast cleaning cycles minimise the production interruption between colour batches, and integrated sieving with automatic fresh powder addition keeps the powder supply balanced throughout the run. Plants operating job-shop production with high colour diversity see the most significant benefit from this configuration.

## Non-Conductive Walls for Cleaner Operation

One of the most operationally important features of the GEMA Magic System is its non-conductive wall construction. Unlike conventional booths, these walls stay clean during the coating cycle — powder does not adhere to them while the guns are spraying. This reduces cleaning time between colour changes to minutes rather than hours and ensures there is no cross-contamination between batches.

In Indian plants where colour discipline is critical — aluminium extrusion coating, contract coating services, and premium architectural finishing are clear examples — this is a meaningful quality assurance advantage. Reduced cross-contamination directly lowers reject rates and rework, both of which have a measurable impact on production cost per part.

## Flexible Layout and Integration

The GEMA automatic booth is designed to integrate into existing factory buildings without major civil works. The modular layout combines a pre- and post-touch-up zone for operator-assisted corrections with a large automatic application area using [reciprocators](/products/gema/reciprocators) and [automatic guns](/products/gema/automatic-gun), all within a compact overall footprint. This means Indian manufacturers can upgrade from manual to automatic booth operation within their existing facility, which significantly reduces the capital barrier to automation.

The booth integrates natively with GEMA reciprocators, the [OptiCentre](/products/gema/opticentre) powder management system, and GEMA control software — enabling recipe-based operation where gun positions, speeds, and parameters are recalled automatically for each product type. When a new part enters the line, the operator selects the saved recipe and the system configures itself, with no manual adjustment of gun positions or spray parameters needed.

## Powder Recovery and Environmental Compliance

All GEMA automatic booth systems incorporate closed-loop powder recovery. Overspray is captured by the booth's exhaust airflow, separated, sieved, and returned to the supply hopper automatically. Modern GEMA recovery systems achieve high separation efficiency, meaning the overwhelming majority of powder that misses the part is recovered and reused rather than discarded.

For Indian manufacturers where powder cost represents a significant share of coating variable cost, this recovery efficiency has a direct positive impact on margins. The process also generates no VOC emissions and produces minimal solid waste, making automatic GEMA booths straightforwardly compliant with Indian environmental regulations for finishing operations.
